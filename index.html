About the Course

Construction A to Z is a comprehensive practical online training program for civil engineering students, diploma holders, architects, interior designers, site engineers, contractors, supervisors, builders, developers, and construction professionals.

The training follows a complete G+1/G+2 residential bungalow project, explaining the different stages of construction from architectural planning to final completion and handover.
